# Office Hours: the paid wedge

**Mode:** startup diagnostic · pre-product · **Position:** worth testing as a service-led wedge, not yet justified as a platform.

## Six forcing answers
1. **Demand reality:** recurring public complaints and maintained workarounds prove pain, not purchase intent. No evidence yet shows an owner will pay $1,500. The first demand event is a paid setup deposit.
2. **Status quo:** an owner or moderator answers repeat questions, updates a pinned post or Notion page irregularly, and loses the reasoning/source trail. The cost is expert time, degraded member value, and potentially missed affiliate revenue; it is not yet quantified per buyer.
3. **Desperate user:** the owner of a 100–1,000 member, $10K+/month photography, cycling, 3D-printing, music-production, or fitness community who sees the same expensive buying question weekly and already has a trusted senior expert.
4. **Narrowest paid wedge:** a $1,500 human-led sprint that converts one community's archive into ten dated, cited, expert-signed buying guides. Software exists only to review, approve, and maintain those guides.
5. **Observation plan:** watch five owners update one recommendation without coaching. Record where they seek evidence, how they preserve dissent, and whether they invite a second expert. A survey or guided demo does not count.
6. **Future-fit:** as search referrals weaken and paid rooms capture purchase intent, community-specific trust becomes more valuable. If platform-native AI produces trusted, cited, reviewed recommendations, the software layer becomes less valuable; the expert workflow and proprietary update history must survive that shift.

## Premise challenge and approaches
- The outcome is not “a better resource page”; it is fewer repeated questions and a trusted purchase decision that stays current.
- Doing nothing is tolerable for low-value communities, so target only communities where gear questions are frequent and purchases are expensive.
- **A — Concierge sprint (S / low):** generate ten guides manually, deliver in the owner's current tools. Fastest paid proof; weak software learning.
- **B — Maintenance bench (M / medium, chosen):** interactive guide scoring, provenance, dissent, review queue, plus concierge onboarding. Proves the differentiator without platform connectors.
- **C — Community intelligence platform (XL / high):** ingest every thread, affiliate catalog, identity, and paywall. Ideal architecture later; premature before deposits.

## Assignment and success gate
Send 20 personalized teardown Looms. Continue only if at least five owners take calls and three place a paid or refundable deposit for the setup sprint. On calls, ask the owner to update a real guide while observed.
