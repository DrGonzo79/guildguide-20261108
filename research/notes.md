# Source notes

## Live report
- **Title:** Software that turns community expertise into maintained buying guides for paid membership groups
- **Report date / URL:** 2026-11-08 · https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/today
- **IB Index:** 7.0 · **Potential:** $3M–$5M ARR · **Difficulty:** moderate · **Capital:** $5K–$15K · **First revenue:** 60–90 days
- **Buyer:** owners of paid, gear-heavy Skool, Circle, Discord, forum, and Mighty communities that already maintain pinned recommendations, spreadsheets, or resource pages.
- **Painful status quo:** repeat purchase questions, stale pinned posts, and shared Notion pages without review dates, provenance, disagreement, or paywall-aware controls.
- **Wedge:** structured, expert-reviewed guides built from existing discussions; visible disagreements, original-source citations, dated reviews, member voting, and access behind the community's existing paywall.

## Market and evidence
The report cites 15,900 paid Skool communities, a $29/month median membership, about 2,600 communities above $10K/month, and 8,400 with affiliate programs. It also reports Skool's claim of 60,000 communities earning $1B/year collectively. These are directional secondary figures, not independently verified here.

Demand receipts repeatedly describe daily duplicate gear questions and outdated lists. The why-now case combines paid-community growth with weakened free buying-guide distribution: reported Wirecutter Google visibility down over 60% May–August 2025, a reported 58% CTR loss when AI Overviews appear, and increasing AI Overview presence on commercial-intent queries.

## Competitor map
- **Notion (incumbent):** flexible page, no maintenance workflow or community-native access controls.
- **Skool / Mighty:** empty classroom or resource containers.
- **Circle:** AI knowledge engine and uploaded docs, not reviewed recommendations.
- **Wallu / Answer Overflow:** FAQ deflection and searchable archives.
- **Wirecutter:** high-trust editorial guide model, but broad/public rather than community-specific.

## Offer ladder and GTM
1. Free 20-minute guide-audit Loom.
2. $1,500 setup sprint producing ten guides in 30 days.
3. $149/month software plus $400/month passed-through expert retainer.
4. Optional 10% affiliate GMV share.

The report models ~400 gear-heavy targets among ~2,300 Skool communities above $10K/month. It assumes 120 cold teardowns in 90 days, 10% replies, 40% reply-to-call, 30% call-to-setup, and 25 customers in year one for roughly $120K booked. Every conversion rate is unvalidated. Distribution is named-owner outbound, not SEO.

## Risks and build direction
Biggest risks are a bundled native module from a community platform, expert-editor churn, the “we already have Notion” objection, and zero direct search demand. The prototype therefore demonstrates the difference: recommendation scoring with provenance, review age, visible dissent, and an actionable maintenance queue—not chat, scraping, affiliate tracking, or a generic wiki.
